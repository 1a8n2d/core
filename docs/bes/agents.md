## Shared Agent Safeguards

- Agents may generate drafts, specifications, internal tasks, and recommendations without external side effects.
- Agents must require explicit user approval before external submissions, contract execution, partner applications, legal acceptance, or financial agreement changes.
- Agents must record provenance for every extracted requirement and generated recommendation.
- Agents must expose confidence, uncertainty, open questions, and dependencies in their outputs.
